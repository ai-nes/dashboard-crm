# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI-NES is used by admissions Sales and Promoter teams while following up on individual Grade 12 prospects. They need to understand a student's readiness, concerns, family context, and next best action quickly enough to make a relevant, timely contact.

## Product Purpose

AI-NES is an admissions intelligence CRM for turning prospect signals into informed enrollment actions. Success means a counselor can move from a student's current context to one clear, appropriate follow-up without hunting through disconnected records.

## Brand Personality

Confident, empathetic, action-oriented. The product should make complex student context feel calm, trustworthy, and ready to act on.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboards that are decorative, metric-first, or overloaded with competing cards and colors. Do not make sensitive student and family information feel clinical, surveillance-like, or gamified.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the decision a counselor needs to make, then show the evidence.
- Treat a student's journey as a coherent story, not a collection of disconnected events.
- Keep personal context human and respectful while preserving operational clarity.
- Make the recommended next action concrete, timely, and easy to take.
- Preserve a calm visual field so urgency is meaningful when it appears.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessibility expectations. Do not communicate status through color alone, support reduced motion, and accommodate Vietnamese labels and variable data lengths.
